Oloid Workflow is a powerful workflow management system that helps organizations streamline their processes and improve efficiency. Data security is a crucial aspect of any workflow management system, and Oloid Workflow offers several features to ensure that your data is safe and protected. ## Encryption

Oloid Workflow encrypts data both at rest and in transit. Oloid uses AWS KMS as its Encryption Key Management System.

## Access Control

Oloid Workflow offers robust access control mechanisms that ensure only authorized users have access to your sensitive data. You can assign different roles and permissions to users based on their job functions, ensuring that they only have access to the information they need.

## Data Backup and Recovery

Oloid Workflow provides a data backup and recovery feature that ensures your data is safe and secure, even in the event of a disaster. With regular backups, you can quickly recover lost or corrupted data, ensuring that your workflows are not disrupted. You can also set up automated backups to ensure that your data is always up-to-date.

Oloid Workflow regularly releases security updates that address vulnerabilities and improve system performance. ## Audit Trails

Oloid Workflow provides detailed audit trails that log all user activity, including who accessed what data and when. This helps you keep track of who's doing what in the system and detect any unauthorized access attempts. Audit trails also help with compliance requirements, such as HIPAA and PCI-DSS.

## Data retention

Oloid stores flow-job-related data for a limited period of time, to provide visibility into system activity, and facilitate testing and debugging.
