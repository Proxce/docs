## Introduction

This document provides step-by-step instructions for logging in with badge and NFC to Workforce / HRMS platform.

## Pre-requisite

- User must have an Okta account.
- In the Oloid Portal, admin must do the following:
  - Okta credential (user email ID) must be added to the user details page in Oloid Portal.
  - Assign badge to the user. For more details, refer [How to assign Badge ID as credential to users](/platform/tenant-admin-portal/how-to-assign-badge-id-as-credential-to-users).
  - Add NFC for the user. For more details, refer [How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to the user](/platform/tenant-admin-portal/how-to-assign-nfc-id-as-a-credential-to-the-user)
  - Enable Badge option under the Oloid Verify application in Authentication tab. For more details, refer [How to create and configure Connect for Passwordless application.](/platforms/ios/connect-passwordless/how-to-create-and-configure-oloid-connect-passwordless-application-in)

## Using the Badge

1. Launch the Oloid Connect app on your iPad.
2. On the home page of the app, select **Badge Auth**.

   ![On the home page of the app, select Badge Auth.](/images/intercom/9891142/image-e6b5ba5c.png)

   The **Scan Badge at the Reader** screen is displayed.

   ![The Scan Badge at the Reader screen is displayed.](/images/intercom/9891142/image-97a2733f.png)

3. Tap the **Badge** on the **Reader** to scan it.

   <Note>
     **Note:** The Card Reader should be connected to your iPad.
   </Note>

4. On Badge authentication, the **Tap NFC Tag at the Device** screen is displayed.

   ![On Badge authentication, the Tap NFC Tag at the Device screen is displayed.](/images/intercom/9891142/image-b0eb2bd8.png)

5. Tap the **NFC tag** on the **Reader** to scan it.

   <Note>
     **Note:** The Reader should be connected to your iPad.
   </Note>

   On NFC authentication, you are signed in to the **Workforce /HRMS** platform.

   ![On NFC authentication, you are signed in to the Workforce /HRMS platform.](/images/intercom/9891142/image-29ac8aa1.png)
