## Introduction

This document explains how a Tenant Admin or Supervisor logs in to Admin Mode in the Oloid Connect app using Microsoft single sign-on (SSO). With Microsoft SSO, you sign in with your organization’s Microsoft credentials instead of entering a separate email and password, which makes first-time setup of Oloid Connect faster and more secure.

## Prerequisites

- You must have a Tenant Admin account.
- The Oloid Connect app must be downloaded and installed from the App Store. For details, refer [How to download Oloid Connect app](/platforms/ios/time-clock/oloid-connect/oloid-connect-how-to-download-and-install-on-ipad).
- SSO must be configured in the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Steps to log in with Microsoft SSO

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect** app.

   ![Launch the Oloid Connect app.](/images/intercom/10293864/image-6f1a1dfb.png)

   The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Pair as Supervisor.**

   ![Tap Pair as Supervisor.](/images/intercom/10293864/ecc40269-285d-4f4e-88e2-f9771a362fd6-9a614ee0.png)

3. Enter **Tenant Name.**
4. Tap **Next**.

   ![Tap Next.](/images/intercom/10293864/image_ip-4b10c381.png)

   TheLogin screen is displayed.
5. Tap **Login with SSO**.

   ![Tap Login with SSO.](/images/intercom/10293864/bccab0d0-afda-4533-91d6-7bbcb81148ee-859f42c1.png)

   The SSO options screen is displayed.
6. Tap **Continue with Microsoft**.

   ![Tap Continue with Microsoft.](/images/intercom/10293864/b71d107a-4cf0-4148-9329-cdeec81cabca-d357b3bd.png)

   The confirmation pop-up box is displayed.
7. Tap **Continue**.

   ![Tap Continue.](/images/intercom/10293864/2d5c1975-c4a9-4be2-9f1f-46a375f47b07-dd1b38ed.png)

   The **Microsoft sign-in** screen is displayed.
8. Do the following:
   1. Enter **Email ID**.
   2. Tap **Next**.

      ![Tap Next. (2)](/images/intercom/10293864/image-7322e753.png)

      The **Enter password** screen is displayed.
9. Do the following:
   1. Enter **Password**.
   2. Tap **Sign in**.

      <Note>
        **Note:** While your Microsoft sign-in session is active, you won’t need to enter your password again for later logins. You may still be asked for your email address.
      </Note>

      ![Tap Sign in.](/images/intercom/10293864/b7358811-3f61-4e89-8f53-33a4a9439b7f-242ba43a.png)

      You are now successfully logged in to Admin Mode in the Oloid Connect app with Microsoft SSO.

![You are now successfully logged in to Admin Mode in the Oloid Connect app with Microsoft SSO.](/images/intercom/10293864/91a4d3c6-378d-473d-ba83-bf2ad7da1dfd-effe245e.png)
