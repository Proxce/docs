## Introduction

Ad-hoc reports, also referred to as Prebuilt reports can be created, saved, and downloaded in the Oloid Portal. Pre-built reports are of 9 types:

[Endpoint Status Report](#endpoint-status-report)

## Prerequisite

You must have admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.

### Endpoint Details Report

An Endpoint Details Report provides detailed information on all the endpoints associated with the application.

Here's how to create and download Endpoint Details reports:

1. Open the Oloid Portal.
2. Navigate to "**Reports**" > "**Ad-Hoc**".
3. Click "**Create & Download Report**."
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to create & download report.
   For detailed instructions, see [How to create & download Endpoint details report](/platform/tenant-admin-portal/tenant-admin-portal-how-to-create-and-download-endpoint-details).
