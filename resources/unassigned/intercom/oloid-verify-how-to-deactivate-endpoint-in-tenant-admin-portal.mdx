## Introduction

This document explains how to deactivate an endpoint in the Tenant Admin Portal.

<Note>
  **Note:** Once you pair an endpoint with Oloid Verify application, it gets saved and appears in the Tenant Admin Portal.
</Note>

## Prerequisite

You must pair Oloid Verify endpoint. For more, refer to[How to Pair an Oloid Verify Endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616605-how-to-pair-an-oloid-verify-endpoint)

## Steps to Deactivate Endpoint

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Endpoints** on the left menu panel.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/15857432/635e3de4-675c-46a8-93f8-37de42aa1b85-013fb478.png)
   The **Endpoints** page is displayed.
3. Select the paired Oloid Verify Endpoint.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/15857432/image-3310ee8b.png)
   The **Endpoint Details** page is displayed.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/15857432/image-926215ad.png)
4. Do the following to deactivate the endpoint:
   1. Click **Action**.
   1. From the drop-down menu, click **Deactivate**.
      ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/15857432/image-95e53ad6.png)
      The **Confirm Deactivation** Pop up box is displayed.
5. Click **Confirm**.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/15857432/image-444938fc.png)
   Oloid Verify Endpoint is deactivated successfully.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/15857432/image-8918a400.png)
