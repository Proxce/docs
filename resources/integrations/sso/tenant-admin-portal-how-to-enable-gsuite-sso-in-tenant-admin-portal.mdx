**Steps to configure GSuite SSO:**

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **user profile icon** on the bottom left corner of the menu panel.
   ![How to Enable GSuite SSO in Tenant... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/11129566/image-ad0dfa51.png)
   The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Click **Tenant Settings**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/11129566/dbb3ba06-85c5-488f-8762-8fdebf21e109-0a65913b.png)
   The **Tenant Settings** page is displayed.
4. Go to **Security** > click **GSuite**.
   ![How to Enable GSuite SSO in Tenant... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/11129566/dd0f11f1-0a73-4f9d-af29-3818a98bf7cf-be474ac0.png)
5. Turn on **Enable GSuite SSO** toggle > click **Save**.
   ![How to Enable GSuite SSO in Tenant... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/11129566/a28d511c-484f-4a41-9d89-515ca9bfd05c-fc1ff694.png)
   The **GSuite** SSO configuration is saved successfully.
   ![How to Enable GSuite SSO in Tenant... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/11129566/image-ac78dc71.png)
