## Steps to create Secrets

1. On the Tenant Admin homepage click **Home** > **Secrets**.
2. On the Secrets list page click **Create Secrets**.
3. Enter **Secret name**, **Key Value pair** > click **Save**.
   The Secret is successfully created.
