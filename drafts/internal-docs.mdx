## Access rules

- **Public docs**: Platforms & Solutions, Integrations, OLOID Platform, Help & Reference, More Resources, and the other Drafts collections. No login needed.
- **Internal docs**: Unassigned and Excluded drafts. Requires sign-in and membership in the `oloid` or `test` group.
- If you open an internal link without access, the page shows as not found.
