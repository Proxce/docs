To test network connectivity on the endpoint:

1. Launch your Oloid **Verify/Connect** App.
2. **Double-tap** on the Preview screen with two fingers.

   <Note>
     **Note:** You can see Preview screen once the Oloid Verify App is paired.
   </Note>

   To access endpoint details, refer [Oloid Connect/Verify: How to see the endpoint details on the endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8293952-oloid-connect-verify-how-to-see-the-endpoint-details-on-the-endpoint).
3. Click **YES-CONTINUE** to log into Admin Mode.
   The Settings screen is displayed.
4. Go to the **Settings** page of Oloid **Verify/Connect** app.
5. Tap **Start** in **Health Check** section.
   ![Oloid Connect/Verify: How to test... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8294042/image-6b98d696.png)
   The **Please Select Health Type** pop-up box is displayed.

On the pop-up box, tap **Network Route Status**.

![Oloid Connect/Verify: How to test... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8294042/image-7140877e.png)

The **Network Status** page is displayed.

![Oloid Connect/Verify: How to test... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8294042/image-1ebbf79b.png)
