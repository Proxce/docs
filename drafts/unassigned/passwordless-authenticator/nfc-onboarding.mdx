## Introduction

NFC/IDs offer a secure means of authenticating and identifying users through company-provided NFC badges. NFC onboarding in the Oloid Platform involves assigning NFC to respective users.

With Oloid's Passwordless feature, you can log into Okta securely by scanning your NFC badge.

### Pre-requisite

You should be a Tenant Admin in the Oloid Platform.

### To assign NFC/ID

1. Log in to the Oloid Platform.
   Log in to your account with the appropriate credentials.
2. Locate the "**Directory**" section.
   Navigate to the "**Directory**" section to find various options for managing user profiles and permissions.
3. Navigate to the "**Users**" menu.
   In the "**Directory**" section, select the "**Users**" menu.
4. Assign "**NFC ID"**.
   In the "**Users**" page select the user to whom you want to assign a NFC ID. Then, follow the on-screen instructions to complete the process.
   For a detailed guide on NFC onboarding, refer **[How to assign NFC/ID as credential to users](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8403103-how-to-assign-nfc-id-as-credential-to-users).**
