## Introduction

A QR code is a type of barcode that can be read easily by a digital device and which stores information as a series of pixels in a square-shaped grid. QR Codes provide access to information in a quick and secure way. QR onboarding in Oloid Platform simplifies and improves the onboarding experience for the users.

### Pre-requisite

You should be a Tenant Admin in the Oloid Platform.

### To create QR Code

1. Access the Oloid Platform.
   Log in to your account with the appropriate credentials.
2. Navigate to the "**Users**" menu.
   In the "**Directory**" section, select the "**Users**" menu.
3. Create a QR Code.
   In the "**Users**" page select the user for whom you want to create a QR Code. Then, follow the on-screen instructions to complete the process.
   For detailed guide on QR onboarding, refer **[How to create QR Code as credential for users](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8294162-how-to-create-qr-code-as-credential-to-users).**
