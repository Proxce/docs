**Steps to add Genetec account:**

1. Click **Accounts** on top right of main page.
   The Accounts pop-up box is displayed.
2. Select **Genetec** from the displayed accounts.
   The Add Account pop-up screen is displayed.
   ![Genetec Account - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9360942/image-eb0878ed.png)
3. Do the following:
   1. Enter **Account Name** for the account.
   2. Enter instance **URL**.
   3. Enter **Username or Email** and **Password**.
   4. Click **Test** to test for connectivity (optional but recommended).
   5. Click **Add Account**.
      The account is created and listed on the Accounts page.

## Steps to associate Genetec Account to a Trigger/Action Node

While creating a flow, whenever you are using Genetec action/trigger node, you can associate Genetec account in the second tab of node config pop-up box.
