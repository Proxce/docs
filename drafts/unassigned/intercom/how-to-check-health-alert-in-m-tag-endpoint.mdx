Steps to check health alert.

1. Login to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **Endpoints** from the left menu panel.
   The **Endpoints** page is displayed.
3. Click M-tag Endpoint name to edit
   The **Endpoint detail**s page is displayed.
4. On the top right corner, locate **Health**.
5. Do the following:
   1. Enter the recipient's email id(s) in **Send Health Alerts to** field.
   2. Turn on **Enable Heath Alerts** toggle button to receive health alerts of the endpoint at your configured email.
   3. Click **Save Email**.
      The health alert setup is configured. The email recipient receives the health alerts.
