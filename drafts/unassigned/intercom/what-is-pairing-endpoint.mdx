Pairing Endpoint (Endpoint Creation)

Endpoints are the devices that facilitate specific functionalities within an application. Each endpoint is associated with the application, and this association is commonly referred to as "pairing an endpoint."

Pairing Device ( Windows, iPad, iPhone, Tablets, android phones) involves associating endpoints with respective Oloid application.

An application is mapped to connections in the Oloid Platform. Pairing devices ensures that the right users within the mapped connections can effectively utilize the associated endpoint and application.

Pairing an endpoint automatically creates endpoint ID in the Oloid portal and the endpoint details are added to the Endpoint section of Oloid Portal.
