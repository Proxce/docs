On the Oloid Verify app, you can share logs with the app developer in two ways:
[View and Share Logs with the App Developer](#view-and-share-logs-with-the-app-developer)
[Upload Logs to the App Developer](#upload-logs-to-the-app-developer)

## Prerequisite

Endpoint configuration for debugLog should be done in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more details, refer to [How to Enable DebugLog](https://oloid.help/en/articles/10511068-how-to-configure-endpoint-for-oloid-verify#:~:text=Enable%20Generate%20Debug%20Logs%20to%20activate%20log%20generation%20for%20the%20endpoint%20until%20disabled.)

## View and Share Logs with the App Developer

### Steps to View and Share Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify** app.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more details, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Verify endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616605-how-to-pair-an-oloid-verify-endpoint)
   </Note>

2. Tap the screen four times using a single finger.
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/13406491/image-c732507b.png)
   The **OnDevice Sync Report** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Tap **VIEW LOGS.**
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/13406491/image-ec92e99a.png)
   The log report is displayed on the screen.
4. On the top right corner of the report page, tap share button to send the log report to the developer.
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/13406491/image-4747b2be.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:** use **AirDrop** to transfer the logs to another iPad or iOS device where the Mail app is configured. Once transferred, you can email the logs from that device.
   </Note>

   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/13406491/image-93914832.png)

## Upload Logs to the App Developer

### Steps to Upload Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify App**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more details, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Verify endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616605-how-to-pair-an-oloid-verify-endpoint)
   </Note>

   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/13406491/image-46fc3e73.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **YES-CONTINUE.**
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/13406491/image-739da5c8.png)
   The **Settings** screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Start** in the **Health Check** section.
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/13406491/image-3a22f1e6.png)
   The **Please Select Health Type** pop-up box is displayed.
5. Tap **Upload Logs.**
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 8](/images/intercom/13406491/image-315d90c2.png)
   The logs are successfully uploaded and automatically shared with the app developer.
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 9](/images/intercom/13406491/image-58a4bd08.png)
