These videos support:

- Feature walkthroughs
- Step-by-step configuration guides

**Video Format**

- Length: **1–2 minutes**
- Style: **Screen recording with AI voiceover**
- Voice generation: **Clueso**
- Post-production: **Canva**
- Audience: **Mixed (Tenant Admins, IT Admins, Support teams)**

All videos must be **clear, concise, technically accurate, and aligned with Oloid’s documentation standards.**

## 1️⃣ Recording Guidelines

### 🎥 Screen Recording Standards

- Record in **Full HD (1920 × 1080 or similar proportion)**.
- Use **100% browser zoom** unless UI clarity requires adjustment.
- Use a **clean demo environment** with no irrelevant data.
- Close unnecessary tabs and disable notifications before recording.

---

### 🖱 Cursor Movement

Cursor movement should guide the viewer’s attention clearly.

- Move the cursor **deliberately and slowly**.
- **Pause briefly before clicking** to highlight the action.
- Avoid rapid or erratic movements.
- Keep the cursor focused on the **active UI area**.

---

### 🔎 Visual Focus

- Use zoom **only when necessary**.
- Avoid excessive zoom effects.
- Keep transitions **minimal and clean**.
- Ensure **UI text is clearly readable** throughout the recording.

## 2️⃣ Script Guidelines

Micro videos (1–2 minutes) must be **tightly structured and concise**. Avoid improvisation.

---

### 🔹 Objective First

Start the video with a clear outcome statement.

**Format**

“Welcome to the video tutorial on how to configure [Feature Name] in the Tenant Admin Portal.”

Keep the introduction direct and instructional.
Avoid marketing language.

---

### 🔹 Standard Script Structure (Micro Guide)

### 1. Introduction (10–15 seconds)

- State what the user will accomplish.
- Mention the system area being used.

Example:

“In this video, we’ll walk through how to configure Windows Login settings in the Tenant Admin Portal.”

---

### 2. Prerequisite Reminder (Optional – ~5 seconds)

Include only when required for the task.

Example:

“Ensure you have Tenant Admin access before proceeding.”

---

### 3. Step-by-Step Walkthrough (60–90 seconds)

- Follow the **logical UI order**.
- Use **one action per sentence**.
- Match **exact UI labels**.
- Avoid filler phrases such as “basically,” “simply,” or “just.”

---

### 4. Outcome Statement (10 seconds)

End the video by reinforcing the expected result.

Example:

“Once configured, users can securely authenticate using their assigned credentials.”

---

### 🔹 Script Writing Rules

✅ Use **second person (“you”)**
✅ Keep sentences **short and clear**
✅ Use **exact product terminology**
✅ Define acronyms on first use
✅ Mirror official documentation steps

❌ Avoid marketing language
❌ Avoid filler phrases
❌ Avoid overly long sentences

---

## 🎙 Voiceover Standards (Clueso AI Generated)

All Oloid Help Center videos use **AI voice generation in Clueso**.

Voiceovers are created by **adapting approved documentation scripts** into a concise narration suitable for video demonstrations.

---

### 🔹 Script Source

- Start with **approved Help Center documentation**.
- Do **not change the logic or steps**.
- Adjust wording slightly to make it **natural for spoken delivery**.
- Ensure terminology **matches the product UI exactly**.

The video must accurately reflect the documentation.

---

### 🔹 Tone Calibration for AI Voice

Even though narration is AI-generated, it must sound:

- **Professional**
- **Neutral**
- **Confident**
- **Clear**

Use **Matilda’s voice in Clueso**.

Avoid:

- Overly conversational tone
- Marketing phrases
- Excited emphasis
- Humor or informal expressions

## 3️⃣ Editing & Post-Production Guidelines (Canva Workflow)

All Oloid Help Center videos follow this post-production workflow:

1. Screen recording is completed
2. AI voiceover is generated in **Clueso**
3. The exported video is imported into **Canva**
4. Final edits and visual elements are applied in Canva

Post-production should **enhance clarity without distracting from the instructional content**.

---

### 🔹 Standard Editing Flow

### Step 1: Import to Canva

- Upload the **final screen recording with AI voiceover**.
- Trim unnecessary silence at the **beginning and end**.
- Ensure the **cursor movement and narration are properly synced**.

---

### 🔹 Intro Slide (5–7 seconds)

Purpose: Establish the topic and maintain branding consistency.

Include:

- Feature name (exact product terminology)
- Oloid branding (consistent logo placement)
- Clean, minimal background

Keep the intro **simple and professional**.

---

### 🔹 On-Screen Pointers & Highlights

Pointers help guide viewer attention.

Pointers should:

- Highlight specific UI elements.
- Appear **exactly when referenced in narration**.
- Remain **clean and subtle**.

Avoid excessive animation or distracting effects.

---

### 🔹 Background Music

Standardized music is added in Canva.

**Music track:** *Positive Ideas*

Settings:

- Reduce music volume to **5**
- Increase narration volume to **150**

Music should remain **subtle and ambient** and must **never compete with narration**.

Avoid dramatic or energetic tracks.

---

### 🔹 Outro Slide (5–7 seconds)

Purpose: Provide closure and guide users to documentation.

Standard format:

“Contact Oloid support for any query - Support@oloid.ai”

Include:

- Oloid branding
- Clean background

Avoid promotional messaging.

---

### 🔹 Visual Cleanliness Standards

Before exporting the final video, verify:

✅ No personal or sensitive data visible
✅ Sensitive information blurred in **Clueso during voice sync**
✅ Demo environment appears clean
✅ No notifications or pop-ups
✅ Cursor movement appears controlled and intentional

---

### 🔹 Audio-Visual Sync Check

Before final export from Canva:

- Narration aligns with cursor movement
- No narration plays before the visual action occurs
- No lag between clicks and explanations
- No abrupt audio cuts
