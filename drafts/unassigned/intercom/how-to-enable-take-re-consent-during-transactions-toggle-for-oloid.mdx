## Steps to enable Take Re-consent During Transactions Toggle

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the **Applications** page, click on the Oloid Connect application.
   ![How to Enable Take Re-consent During... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/12958185/9f89c7a2-1c62-4a42-b386-2859701842fc-99aa4506.png)
   The application page is displayed.
   ![How to Enable Take Re-consent During... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/12958185/image-ff7a1de6.png)
3. Go to **Authenticator** > **Face** tab.
   ![How to Enable Take Re-consent During... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/12958185/c2e7c361-146a-407f-8a83-651539b06129-c482ae4a.png)
4. Turn on **Take Re-consent During Transactions** toggle button.
   ![How to Enable Take Re-consent During... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/12958185/83c63ec9-3ac3-4f11-83ba-335ee732d112-263dcfce.png)
5. Click **Save**.
   ![How to Enable Take Re-consent During... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/12958185/3797d9ba-ca19-46c0-890b-06f3c1c347df-fdc3d829.png)
   The configuration is saved successfully.
