## Introduction

This document explains how to onboard a Face credential using Okta Single Sign-On (SSO) with Custom URL. It guides you through the login process using Okta and the steps required to access the Face Credential page.

## Prerequisites

- Ensure that the Custom URL is available.
- Ensure that valid Okta user credentials are available.

## Steps to Onboard Face using Okta SSO

1. Lauch the **Custom URL**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Refer to the following custom URL:
   </Note>

   `https://**tenantname.myoloid**.com/ssologin?sso=okta&section=face`
   The **Okta Sign In** screen is displayed.
2. Enter Okta **Username** and click **Next**.
   ![Onboard Face Using Okta SSO with Custom... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/14717359/image-a95d65ae.png)
   The **Password** screen is displayed.
3. Enter Okta **Password** and click **Sign In**.
   ![Onboard Face Using Okta SSO with Custom... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/14717359/image-cf21c348.png)
   The **Face Credential** page is displayed.
4. Click **Submit Consent**.
   **Note: Submit Consent** is available only when consent is enabled.
   ![Onboard Face Using Okta SSO with Custom... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/14717359/image-4f403330.png)
   The Consent form page is displayed.
5. Click **Agree.**
   ![Onboard Face Using Okta SSO with Custom... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/14717359/image-08d7dc45.png)
6. Click **Add Face** > Click **Allow** to provide camera permission if prompted.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Steps 4 sand 5 applicable only when consent is enabled.
   </Note>

   ![Onboard Face Using Okta SSO with Custom... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/14717359/f30a48c9-7164-4637-9513-11aa6f53d364-b25a2f1c.png)
   The camera opens on your device.
7. Position your face in front of the camera and tap the **Capture** button to enroll your face.

   <Note>
     **Note:** If you do not tap the **Capture** button, the app automatically captures your face after a few seconds.
   </Note>

   ![Onboard Face Using Okta SSO with Custom... - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/14717359/image-450d270d.png)
   The pop-up screen appears with the captured image.
8. Do one of the following:
   1. Select **Add Face** to submit the image.
      The face is submitted successfully and is assigned as a face credential.
   2. Select **Retake** to take your photo again.
      The camera turns on to retake the photo.

      <Note>
        **Note:** You can add a maximum of five faces per user.
      </Note>

      Your face should be visible in the camera, or else it might get rejected. It is recommended to avoid wearing things that cover your facial features such as sunglasses, masks, headbands, etc.
      ![Onboard Face Using Okta SSO with Custom... - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/14717359/image-21b0540a.png)
      The face is added successfully.
