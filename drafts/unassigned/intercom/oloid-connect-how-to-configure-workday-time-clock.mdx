## Steps to Configure Workday Time Clock

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the **Applications** page, click on the preferred Oloid Connect application.
   ![How to Configure Workday Time Clock - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/15642958/9f89c7a2-1c62-4a42-b386-2859701842fc-99aa4506.png)
   The application **details** page is displayed.
   ![How to Configure Workday Time Clock - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/15642958/image-ff7a1de6.png)
3. Go to **Integrations** tab.
4. Enable **Workday** toggle.
   ![How to Configure Workday Time Clock - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/15642958/image-23253b7e.png)
   The **integration** pop up box is displayed.
5. Click **Save.**
   ![How to Configure Workday Time Clock - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/15642958/image-318143f7.png)
   The Workday integration is enabled successfully
