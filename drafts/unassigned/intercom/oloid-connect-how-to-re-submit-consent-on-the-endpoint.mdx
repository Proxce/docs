## Introduction

If the consent template is updated in the Tenant Admin Portal after a face credential is added, the previously submitted consent becomes outdated. The admin must submit the updated consent again in the Oloid Connect endpoint.

## Prerequisite

Face credential must be added for the user. For more, refer to

[Oloid Connect - Add Face](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8536726-oloid-connect-add-face).

## Step to Re-Submit Consent on the Endpoint

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect** ![How to Re-Submit Consent on the Endpoint - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/14594718/image-6def886f.png) app.

   <Note>
     **Note:** For more information about how to log in to the Oloid Connect, refer to [How to log in to the Admin mode in Oloid Connect App.](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8671144-how-to-log-in-to-the-admin-mode-in-oloid-connect-app)
   </Note>

   The **Settings** page is displayed.
2. Tap **Users** on the menu at the bottom of the screen.
   ![How to Re-Submit Consent on the Endpoint - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/14594718/image-6c2feb88.png)
   The **Users** page with list of users is displayed.
3. Tap on the selected user to Re-Submit consent, or search for a user using the search bar at the top.
   ![How to Re-Submit Consent on the Endpoint - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/14594718/f4cfd404-3251-4290-9302-d9cc154da41a-d9d07a48.png)
   The Details screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Face** tab > Tap **Submit Consent.**
   ![How to Re-Submit Consent on the Endpoint - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/14594718/image-5d73fe02.png)
   The consent form is displayed.

Tap **Accept**.

![How to Re-Submit Consent on the Endpoint - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/14594718/image-6cb12a48.png)

The consent is submitted successfully.

![How to Re-Submit Consent on the Endpoint - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/14594718/image-240e50e8.png)
