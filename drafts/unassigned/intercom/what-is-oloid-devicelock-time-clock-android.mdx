The Oloid Device Lock app in **Time Clocking** mode transforms an Android device into a secure and contactless time clocking system. In this mode, employees authenticate themselves using supported methods such as **Face Authenticator, PIN, Password, Badge, or NFC**. Once authenticated, their **clock-in and clock-out times are automatically recorded**, enabling accurate and efficient attendance tracking for workforce management.
