To enable **Okta Login** on Supervisor application, you need to raise a ticket with [Oloid Support.](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) Your Okta Login will be enabled within 48 hrs.

**Pre-requisite**

You should have supervisor or tenant admin role in the Supervisor app.

Follow the below steps to log into Supervisor app using **Okta SSO Login** option.

1. Launch **Supervisor** app.
   The Supervisor app is displayed.
2. Enter **Tenant Name**.
   ![How to log in to Supervisor app with... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9553064/image-419ee789.png)
3. Select **Next**.
   The **Pair Endpoint** screen is displayed.
4. Select **Pair as Supervisor**.
5. Click **Login with SSO.**
   The configured Login options are displayed.
6. Select **Login with Okta** to log into Supervisor app.
