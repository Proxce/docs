**Pre-requisite:**

You must open admin mode in the Oloid Verify app. See **[How to log into the admin mode of Oloid Verify App as a Supervisor](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8671139-how-to-log-into-the-admin-mode-of-oloid-verify-app-as-a-supervisor)** to know more.

Steps to login with Okta SSO:

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify App** .
   The login screen is displayed.
2. Click on **LOGIN WITH SSO**.
   ![How to login with Okta SSO in Oloid... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/10255776/e3557f1a-7dad-4705-9c69-d26eba961a54-0258f9a6.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:** You get **LOGIN WITH SSO** option only when the endpoint is paired. Check out **[How to pair an Oloid Verify endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616605-how-to-pair-an-oloid-verify-endpoint)** to know more.
   </Note>

   The screen with **SSO options** is displayed.
3. Select **Continue with Okta**.
