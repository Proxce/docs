## Steps to enable Show Camera Flip Button

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the **Applications** page, click on the Oloid Connect application.
   ![How to Enable Show Camera Flip Button... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/12965245/9f89c7a2-1c62-4a42-b386-2859701842fc-99aa4506.png)
   The application page is displayed.
   ![How to Enable Show Camera Flip Button... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/12965245/image-ff7a1de6.png)
3. Go to **Authenticator** > **QR Code** tab.
   ![How to Enable Show Camera Flip Button... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/12965245/7eac3387-6ec2-4792-b67e-e497d5f168c4-1d06f7ba.png)
4. Turn on **Show Camera Flip Button** toggle button.
   ![How to Enable Show Camera Flip Button... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/12965245/2be0797d-f81e-46fa-b223-7f110f69fd57-9a9b3571.png)
5. Click **Save**.
   ![How to Enable Show Camera Flip Button... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/12965245/0e93ae89-ec42-417f-938c-1a76f5d92b17-f929bfd7.png)
   The configuration is saved successfully.
