You need to integrate your tenant to Okta instance to connect to Okta portal.

**Prerequisites:** Before you begin, make sure you have the following prerequisites in place:

1. An active Oloid tenant.
   Your Oloid tenant must be up and running, and you should have the necessary administrative access to configure it.
2. An active Okta instance with administrative privileges.
   Make sure your Okta instance is active and that you have administrative privileges to configure the integration seamlessly.

Refer the following for Okta set up process.

1. **[Okta Settings for Oloid App to Login with Okta](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8159402-okta-settings-for-oloid-app-to-login-with-okta)**
