## Mobile Keys

If a valid Bluetooth token is present, mobile key is shown as enabled.

If the Bluetooth token is missing, mobile key is shown as disabled

## Enable Notification

You can enable notifications to receive messages from the app.

## Set Auto Unlock Mode

Auto Unlock feature of OloID app, allows you to unlock the door without opening the app.

<Note>
  **Note:** Your Bluetooth should be turned on for auto unlock feature to function.
</Note>

You can enable any one of the auto-unlock functions:

- **Background Unlock**: Enabling background unlock in your app opens the door automatically as you are within the range of M-Tag.
- **Push Notification Unlock**: Enabling push notification, you get notifications on nearby doors with M-tag. If using an **iOS phone**, long press on notification and press on **Unlock** to open the door. If using an **Android phone**, press **Unlock** to open the door.
- **Hold to Unlock**: Enabling hold to unlock opens the door as you hold your hand in front of M-Tag to unlock the door.

## Share App Feedback

Tap **Share App Feedback** under **Profile** tab on the menu at the bottom of the screen to share your feedback for the application. The pop-up box is displayed to share feedback.

![Using the OloID App Profile (TBA) - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/6894976/image-614b5678.png)

You can do the following:

1. **Report a bug:** You can report issues you are facing with the app like something in the app is broken or does not work as expected.
2. **Suggest an Improvement**: You can give your new ideas or suggest enhancements for the app.
3. **Ask a question:** You can ask your queries or support on the app.

## View Settings

Tap **Settings** under the **Profile** tab on the menu at the bottom of the screen to view **Settings** screen.

You can do the following:

1. Tap **Share Logs with developer** to send the logs to the developer.
2. Tap **Logout** to exit the application.
