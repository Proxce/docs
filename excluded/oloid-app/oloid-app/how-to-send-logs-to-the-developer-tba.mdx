To share logs with developer:

1. Launch the **OloID app.**
2. Log in using your registered email ID.
   ![How to send logs to the developer (TBA) - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8170508/image-0fe4e764.png)
   The **Wallet** page with a list of doors is displayed.
   ![How to send logs to the developer (TBA) - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8170508/image-23fb7d1a.png)
3. Tap on the **Profile tab** at the bottom right of the screen.
   ![How to send logs to the developer (TBA) - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8170508/image-33a5bc6d.png)
4. Scroll down the bottom of the screen and tap **Settings**.
   The **Settings page** is displayed.
5. On the Settings screen, click on **Share logs with developer**.
   ![How to send logs to the developer (TBA) - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8170508/image-3f70f152.png)
   The logs are uploaded and shared with the developer.
