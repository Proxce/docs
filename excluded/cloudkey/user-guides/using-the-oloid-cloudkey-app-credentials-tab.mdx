To view Credentials tab:

1. Launch the **OLOID CloudKey** app.
2. Tap **Credentials** on the menu at the bottom of the screen to see your credentials.
   ![Using the OloID CloudKey App... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9316981/5fb3ad20-9cd9-4e08-807e-9b1be0ff6db4-8ca196e5.png)
   The **Credentials** screen is displayed, showing the status of the CloudKey credential card.
   ![Using the OloID CloudKey App... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9316981/c8660d48-0b67-43d4-9831-785e306af9c6-7d6d6678.png)

<Note>
  **Note:** The CloudKey credential card is not displayed if it is not generated. The card status shows up as inactive if the credential has expired.
</Note>

Contact **Tenant Admin** to generate CloudKey credential card or extend the expiry date.
