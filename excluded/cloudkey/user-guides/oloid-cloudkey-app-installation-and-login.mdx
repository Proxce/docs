OloID CloudKey app provides an easy contactless way to authenticate and provide secure access over your mobile. With the app, you can easily unlock the door with a tap.

OloID CloudKey app is compatible with all mobile devices. Below is a condensed list of compatible devices.

- Android phones with OS 6 and above
- iPhone with OS 12 and above

**Pre-requisite**: You should have a valid OloID account.

## Installation Procedure

1. Go to **App Store** or **Play Store** and download **OloID CloudKey App.**
2. Install the application and upon successful installation, open the app.
3. Click **Get Started**.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9310637/image-91c5cc53.png)
4. Enter **Email ID** and Click **Next** on the **Sign In** screen.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9310637/b1a736e0-dc03-4359-9454-6ef05562dc56-6cb8c674.png)
5. Enter **Company Code** and click **Apply**.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9310637/d9bcdcad-dd21-45f3-9b78-eec1a019f279-d42e3d23.png)
   The Tenant is listed on the screen.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/9310637/image-400ceaa8.png)
6. Tap on the **Tenant** and click **Apply**.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/9310637/image-0299ec0c.png)
7. Enter your registered email id and click **Next**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** You can also use SSO login.
   </Note>

   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/9310637/image-d3eaecb9.png)
   A 6-digit code is sent to the registered email.
8. Enter the **6-digit code** and tap **Next** to successfully log in.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/9310637/image-57bbd658.png)
9. Click **Continue** when OloID CloudKey App prompts for Bluetooth, location, and push notification permissions.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 8](/images/intercom/9310637/image-16e40f1f.png)
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 9](/images/intercom/9310637/image-50a70bfd.png)
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 10](/images/intercom/9310637/image-ccbbd72e.png)
   The Welcome screen is displayed.
   ![OloID CloudKey App Installation and... - screenshot 11](/images/intercom/9310637/WelcomeScreen-dc971285.png)
   The **Wallet** screen is displayed.
