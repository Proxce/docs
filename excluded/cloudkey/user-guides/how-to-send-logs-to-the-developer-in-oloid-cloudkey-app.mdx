To share logs with developer:

1. Launch the **OLOID CloudKey app.**
2. Log in using your registered email ID.
   ![How to send logs to the developer in... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9316986/image-0fe4e764.png)
   The **Wallet** screen with a list of doors is displayed.
   ![How to send logs to the developer in... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9316986/image-17619f46.png)
3. Tap on the **Profile tab** at the bottom right of the screen.
   ![How to send logs to the developer in... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9316986/image-d338c418.png)
4. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen and tap **Settings**.
   ![How to send logs to the developer in... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/9316986/915485a0-fad7-487f-8cdc-2618ac3b089a-8847be72.png)
   The **Settings** screen is displayed.
5. On the Settings screen, tap **Share logs with developer**.
   ![How to send logs to the developer in... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/9316986/73a5d79a-c5cb-4b62-9c9a-c8c3a720719a-4ee5e365.png)
   The logs are uploaded and shared with the developer.
