## Problem: M-Tag Name is not showing in the App.

When the App is launched and user is near the door (within the required range), the M-Tag name (door name) doesn’t show up.

## Probable Cause

1. Bluetooth is disabled on the phone
2. M-Tag has run out of the battery
3. Hardware issue

## Solution

1. Check if Bluetooth is enabled on the phone. In case Bluetooth is disabled in the phone, user should see a banner on top to enable Bluetooth. Enable the Bluetooth and then try again.
2. If Bluetooth is enabled in the phone and still unable to view the M-Tag name (door name), there is a possibility that the M-Tag has run out of battery. Try replacing the battery by following the below steps –
   a. Remove the Oloid Case.
   b. Remove the M-Tag.
   c. Remove the back panel of the M-Tag.
   d. Take out the battery carefully and insert a new one.
   e. Replace the back panel of the M-Tag and place it in the Oloid case.
   f. Put back the Oloid case on the RFID reader.
3. Now try again.
   If the app still fails to show the M-Tag name (door name), contact [support@oloid.ai](mailto:support@oloid.ai)
