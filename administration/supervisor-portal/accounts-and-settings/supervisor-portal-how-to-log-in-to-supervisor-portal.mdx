The Supervisor Portal is a restricted area within Oloid's Tenant Admin Portal, offering supervisors limited access as defined by the admin.

**Pre-requisite**: You should be a user with Supervisor role.

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar. Then, press **Enter** on your keyboard.
   The Supervisor Portal login page is displayed.
   ![How to log in to Supervisor Portal - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9690901/image-3dc118ef.png)
3. Do the following:
   1. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
   2. Enter **Password**.
   3. Click **Login**.
      The Oloid Supervisor Portal is displayed.

      <Note>
        **Note:** Oloid uses **SSO login** and also allows you to log in to the portal with your Google, Microsoft, Okta, or with your Email account.
      </Note>
