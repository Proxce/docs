A Tenant Admin has full access to the Oloid Platform and all Oloid-connected devices.

Prerequisite: You must be a Tenant Admin .

To set up a Supervisor profile:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Platform**.
2. Click **User profile** icon.
3. Select **Tenant Settings > Admin Controls** tab > **Supervisors**.
4. Click **Invite Supervisor**.
   The **Invite Tenant Supervisor** pop-up box is displayed. Now, follow the on-screen instructions to complete the process.
