Steps to deactivate OloID Account of an User:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal**.
   The **Users** page is displayed.
2. Click username to edit.
   ![How to deactivate OloID Account of a... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9674152/image-444c5f8c.png)
   The user details page is displayed.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9674152/2b89be15-4a92-456f-863c-97ec558d2b94-ef943958.png)
3. On the top-right, go to **Action** > **Deactivate Oloid Account**.
   ![How to deactivate OloID Account of a... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9674152/image-47f0130a.png)
   A confirmation pop-up box is displayed.
   ![How to deactivate OloID Account of a... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9674152/image-b4f27b1f.png)
4. Select **Block**.
   The user's Oloid account is removed successfully.

   <Note>
     **Note:** To recover your account you must create Oloid account.
   </Note>

   For more information, see **How to create OloID account.**
