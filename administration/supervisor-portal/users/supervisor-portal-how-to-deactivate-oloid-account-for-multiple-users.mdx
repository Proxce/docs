Steps to de-activate Oloid Account:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal**.
   The **Users** page is displayed.
2. Select user(s) to de-activate Oloid Account.
   ![How to deactivate Oloid Account for... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9711899/image-d1ec3e03.png)
3. Go to **Action > De-Activate Oloid Account**.
   ![How to deactivate Oloid Account for... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9711899/image-4a9ee64f.png)
   The Oloid account is deactivated for the selected users.
   ![How to deactivate Oloid Account for... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9711899/image-437c42f4.png)
