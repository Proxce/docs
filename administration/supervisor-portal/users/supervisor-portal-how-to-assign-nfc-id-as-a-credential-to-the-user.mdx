### Introduction

NFC/ID is assigned for the DeviceLock app to function.

Steps to assign NFC/ID:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.**
   The Users page is displayed.
2. Click username to edit.
   ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9673945/image-d0cf9511.png)
   The user details page is displayed.
   ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9673945/image-f77afc39.png)
3. Do one of the following:
   1. Go to **Credentials** > **NFC/ID** tab> **+ Assign NFC/ID**.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9673945/63d2da2a-b444-4c24-81e0-a18d463fd2d1-bbccc1a2.png)
   2. Go to **Assign Credential** > **Assign NFC/ID**.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9673945/0b119485-bad6-4c12-addd-237af25e30f7-d3ffc39e.png)
      The **Assign NFC/ID** pop-up box is displayed.
      ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9673945/image-05f6f099.png)
4. On the pop-up box, do the following:
   1. Enter your unique **NFC/ID** security number.

      <Note>
        **Note:** You can tap the **NFC/ID** tag on the **Reader** to scan it.
      </Note>

Click **Assign**.

The NFC/ID number is assigned and listed on the **NFC/ID** tab.

![Image](/images/intercom/9673945/8c9c1c38-9d02-47d0-8ff4-cc5bf302c857-5e861e85.png)
