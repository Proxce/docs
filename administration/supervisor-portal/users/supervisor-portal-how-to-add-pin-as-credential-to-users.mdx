Steps to create PIN:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.**
   The **Users** page is displayed.
2. Click **username** to edit.
   ![How to add PIN as credential to Users - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9674277/image-444c5f8c.png)
   The user details page is displayed.
   ![How to add PIN as credential to Users - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9674277/image-e135574f.png)
3. Do one of the following:
   1. Go to **Credentials** > **PIN** > **Add PIN**.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9674277/0845e845-5a32-432f-a7fb-ae72671110f6-0ed0c80c.png)
   2. Go to **Assign Credential** > **Add PIN**.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9674277/9a3fc86f-1551-43d9-ab9d-c1ca1c6ea154-d914174a.png)
      The **Add PIN** pop-up box is displayed.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9674277/deb77410-e69d-4a21-a79f-db1a1da950fb-527ca6fd.png)
4. On the pop-up box, do the following:
   1. Enter **new PIN**.

      <Note>
        **Note:** You must enter numbers.
      </Note>

   2. Re-enter **new PIN**.
   3. Under **Share PIN With** enter email address you want to share the **pin** with.
   4. Click **Save**.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9674277/a8ee2c05-58fd-4cc6-bb53-652b49793366-ad819783.png)
      The PIN is created and listed on the **PIN** tab.
