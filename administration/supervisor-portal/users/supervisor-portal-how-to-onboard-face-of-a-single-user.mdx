Steps to add face of a single user:

1. Log in to the **Supervisor Portal.**
   The **Users** page is displayed.
2. Click **username** to edit.
   ![How to onboard face of a single user - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9669583/image-22cb7d27.png)
   Theuser details page is displayed.
   ![How to onboard face of a single user - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9669583/image-bf5f47b1.png)
3. Do one of the following:
   1. Go to **Credentials** > **Face** > + **Add Face**.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9669583/7c30a6b4-7827-4749-b466-529867ddc916-b0332b86.png)
   2. Go to **Assign Credential** > **Add Face**.
      ![Image](/images/intercom/9669583/1ed1dfbe-5726-4732-8cc1-4eddd11f28b8-fbbc6000.png)
      The pop-up box to upload image is displayed.
      ![How to onboard face of a single user - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9669583/image-adf47b7b.png)
4. Click or Drag and Drop the image to be uploaded.

Click **Upload**.

The image is uploaded and listed on the **Face** tab.

![How to onboard face of a single user - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/9669583/image-97f06c47.png)

<Note>
  **Note:** You can add a maximum of five faces per user.
</Note>
