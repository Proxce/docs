## Prerequisites

- You must have access to the Tenant Admin Portal.
- Ensure that Permanent Badge is assigned to the user.
- Ensure the badge reader is connected.
- Ensure that the Temporary Badge Toggle is enabled. To enable, refer[How to Enable Temporary Badge in the Tenant Admin Portal](https://oloid.help/en/articles/14653923-how-to-enable-temporary-badge-in-the-tenant-admin-portal-video-tutorial)
