## Introduction

This document provides instructions to log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal** using your Oloid account credentials.

## Prerequisite

You must be a user with Tenant Admin role.

## Steps to log in to the Tenant Admin Portal

1. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar, and then press **Enter**.
   The Tenant Admin Portal **Login** page is displayed.
2. Do the following:
   1. Enter your **Username** or **Email**.
   2. Enter **Password**.
   3. Click **Login**.

      <Note>
        **Note:** You can also log in to the Tenant Admin Portal using a configured SSO or passwordless authentication method, such as, [Google](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8727486-enabling-google-sso), [Microsoft](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8727230-enabling-azure-sso), [Okta](https://oloid.help/en/articles/11744118-how-to-log-in-to-the-tenant-admin-portal-with-okta-sso), [PingOne](https://oloid.help/en/articles/11136469-how-to-log-in-to-oloid-portal-with-pingone-sso), [Auth0](https://oloid.help/en/articles/11130515-how-to-log-in-to-oloid-portal-using-auth0-sso), [Duo SSO](https://oloid.help/en/articles/12147243-how-to-log-in-to-tenant-admin-portal-with-duo-sso), [Personal Passkey,](https://oloid.help/en/articles/15531507-tenant-admin-portal-how-to-log-into-tenant-admin-portal-using-personal-passkey) [Webkey SSO](https://oloid.help/en/articles/13597429-webkey-how-to-login-to-tenant-admin-portal-with-oloid-webkey-sso) with your Email account.
      </Note>
