Resetting your password in the Tenant Admin Portal allows you to create a new password if you've forgotten the current one or need to update it for security. The process includes verifying your identity, receiving a reset link or code, setting a new password, and logging in securely.

**Pre-requisite**

- You should be a user in the Tenant Admin Portal with **Admin** role.

**Follow the below steps to reset password :**

1. Launch your browser.
2. Enter the URL provided for you in the browser’s address bar > press **Enter** on your keyboard.
   The Oloid LOGIN page is displayed.
3. Click **Forgot Password.**
   ![How to reset password in Tenant Admin... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/11411467/image-b76766f2.png)
   The reset password pop-up box is displayed.
4. Do any one of the following:
   1. Enter your registered **Email ID**.
      OR
   2. Select **Country** from drop down menu > Enter your registered **phone number**.
5. Click **Reset**.
   ![How to reset password in Tenant Admin... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/11411467/1fa3e377-07de-412a-9d67-81b348ceadbb-7585a7fd.png)
   A reset password email is sent to the provided email ID.
   ![How to reset password in Tenant Admin... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/11411467/image-89d52d2e.png)
6. Go to your email **inbox >** open the **Oloid Password Reset** email.
7. Click **Reset Password** link.
   ![How to reset password in Tenant Admin... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/11411467/f7a44321-fae6-4b9a-8877-b906b0f6b3f0-1857fbe2.png)
   The Set New Password pop-up box is displayed.
8. Enter your **Email ID**.
9. Enter **Confirmation Code** received on the same email.
10. Enter **New Password**.
11. Click **Login**.
    ![How to reset password in Tenant Admin... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/11411467/image-5f308dcd.png)
    The password is successfully reset in the Tenant Admin Portal. The Tenant Admin Portal home page is displayed.
    ![How to reset password in Tenant Admin... - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/11411467/image-a4d57c63.png)

<Note>
  **Note:** If you are unable to reset your password using the above steps, you may also contact your **Support team** to request a reset password link. Once received, click the link and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the reset process.
</Note>
