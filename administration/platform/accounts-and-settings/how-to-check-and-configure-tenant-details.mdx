## Introduction

The Tenant Admin Portal allows admin to view and configure tenant details. In the portal, you can access tenant information such as tenant name, owner details, and company information.

## Steps to view Tenant details

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the homepage of the portal, click **user profile icon** on the bottom left corner of the menu panel.
   ![How to Check and Configure Tenant... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8810967/image-fd639e0f.png)
   The user profile menu is displayed.
3. Click **Tenant Settings**.
   ![How to Check and Configure Tenant... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8810967/7822a6e4-5bf8-43be-8cc2-a052dbe241e4-26af2bf8.png)
   The **Tenant Settings** page is displayed.
   ![How to Check and Configure Tenant... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8810967/dfcb3827-ef88-47cc-a8a7-a20ee84ece76-f78cb005.png)
4. Do the following under the Tenant **Details** tab:
   1. View **Tenant Name**.
   2. View **Tenant Owner Email**.
   3. View/Edit **Tenant Owner Name**.
   4. View/Edit **Company Name**
   5. Enter **Customer Address**. (Optional)
   6. Click **Save**.
      The Tenant Details are saved.
