Steps to send QR code:

1. Login to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **Directory** from the left menu panel.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9794751/0afd0c1e-17ce-4e1e-90eb-f8bc8d625ed5-0085e038.png)
   The **Users** page is Displayed.
3. Click **username** to edit.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9794751/95620d59-4a46-433f-8818-e0b584d742f6-e9983dec.png)
   The **User details** page is displayed.
   ![How to Send QR Code on Email - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9794751/image-a1835ec6.png)
4. Go to **Credentials** tab > **QR Code** > **Send QR Code on Email**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9794751/e503bb75-fdde-4e28-918a-276f0f5b017f-0bc9367a.png)
   The **Send QR Code on Email** pop-up box is displayed.
   ![How to Send QR Code on Email - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9794751/image-afff5c75.png)
5. On the pop-up screen, do the following :
   1. Enter/Select Email ID from the dropdown list to whom you want to **Share QR Code With**.
   2. Click **Send**.

The QR Code is sent successfully to the email ID.

![How to Send QR Code on Email - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9794751/image-6763c717.png)
