Steps to send invite to download app:

1. Login to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **Directory** from the left menu panel.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9929232/7ca2b378-1db2-446f-9e3f-c4b2ab238133-298c5dcd.png)
   The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Click **username** to edit.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9929232/efbff9cb-f31e-476a-b8a9-7b0d1201df58-5305f90c.png)
   The **User details** page is displayed.
   ![How to send invite to download app to a... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9929232/image-e25c76a6.png)
4. Go to **Credentials** tab > **Bluetooth** > **Invite to Download app**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9929232/926ca0fa-0072-49b2-8a03-069fbbbf2583-1633e19f.png)
   The invite to download app is sent successfully to the user.
   ![How to send invite to download app to a... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9929232/image-92d693a6.png)
