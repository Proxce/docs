Steps to assign NFC/ID:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel.
   ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8403103/image-2124a09a.png)
   The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Click user name to edit.
   ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8403103/image-c5a73cd2.png)
   The user details page is displayed.
   ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8403103/image-c2dd6ec6.png)
4. Do one of the following:
   1. Go to **Credentials** > **NFC/ID** tab> **+ Assign NFC/ID**.
      ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/8403103/image-c348db44.png)
   2. Go to **Assign Credential** > **Assign NFC/ID**.
      ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/8403103/image-4289545f.png)
      The **Assign Badge** pop-up box is displayed.
      ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/8403103/image-dbdb86af.png)
5. On the pop-up box, do the following:
   1. Enter your unique **NFC/ID** security number.

      <Note>
        **Note:** You can tap the **NFC/ID** tag on the **Reader** to scan it.
      </Note>

   2. Click **Assign**.
      The NFC/ID security number is assigned and listed on the **NFC/ID** tab.
      ![How to assign NFC/ID as a credential to... - screenshot 8](/images/intercom/8403103/image-25214d16.png)
