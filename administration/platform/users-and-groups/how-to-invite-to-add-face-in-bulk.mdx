Steps to Invite to Add Face in bulk:

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/8379556/ffd915ca-407a-431b-a0ff-ac003dfa1572-487e74a2.png)
   The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Select **Users.**
4. On top right, Click **Action** > **Send email** > **Invite to add face**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/8379556/cd6936a2-a73a-4de9-af2d-1f05726ebee7-406e04be.png)
   An email invite link is sent to upload their face.
   ![How to invite to add face in bulk - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8379556/image-566cd290.png)
   To upload face using the invite link, see [How to add face using the invite link](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8472935-how-to-add-face-using-the-invite-link)
