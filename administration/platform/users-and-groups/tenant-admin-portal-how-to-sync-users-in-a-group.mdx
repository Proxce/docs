## Introduction

This document provides instructions to sync users from a group to the associated endpoint devices in the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Prerequisite

You must have admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Steps to Sync User

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page, click **Groups** on the left panel menu.
   ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8462702/image-bce7fce4.png)
   The **Groups** page is displayed.
3. Click the preferred **Group Name**.
   ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8462702/image-f0ba9b52.png)
   The **Group Details** page is displayed.
   ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8462702/image-0b17daf8.png)
4. Do one of the following:
   1. Go to **Action** > click **Sync Users**.
      ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8462702/image-3c6cca19.png)
      The **Confirm Action** pop-up box is displayed.
   2. Click **Yes**.
      ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/8462702/image-adbcfdc8.png)
      The users in the group are synced to the associated device(s) successfully.
      ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/8462702/image-e8548d05.png)
      OR
   3. Go to **Users** tab > click **Sync** icon.
      ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/8462702/image-62bfd35e.png)
      The users in the group are synced to the associated endpoint device(s) successfully.
      ![How to Sync Users in a Group - screenshot 8](/images/intercom/8462702/image-cfaf1013.png)
