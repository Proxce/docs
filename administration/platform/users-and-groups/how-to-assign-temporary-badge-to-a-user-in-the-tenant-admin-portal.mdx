## Introduction

This document explains how to assign a Temporary Badge to a user in the Tenant Admin Portal. Temporary Badge allows users to authenticate using a temporary credential when a permanent badge is unavailable or replaced.

## Prerequisites

- You must have access to the Tenant Admin Portal.
- Ensure that Permanent Badge is assigned.
- Ensure the badge reader is connected.
- Ensure that the Temporary Badge Toggle is enabled. To enable, refer[How to Enable Temporary Badge in the Tenant Admin Portal](https://oloid.help/en/articles/14066354-how-to-enable-temporary-badge-in-the-tenant-admin-portal)

## Steps to Assign Temporary Badge to a User

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left menu panel.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/14099788/42812ab0-d4c9-4fc6-9fcd-e2cf4ddd66ce-78fec51b.png)
   The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Click user name to edit.
   ![How to Assign Temporary Badge to a User... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/14099788/image-d98481a2.png)
   The user details page is displayed.
4. Go to **Credentials** tab > **Badge ID** > **+ Scan Badge to Enroll**.
   ![How to Assign Temporary Badge to a User... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/14099788/image-f8ae796e.png)
   The **Scan your Badge** pop-up box is displayed.
5. Select **Assign Temporary Badge** and click **Next**.
   ![How to Assign Temporary Badge to a User... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/14099788/image-34985eec.png)
   The **Scan your Badge** pop-up box is displayed.
6. Scan the **Badge** on the **Badge reader** and click **Save**.
   ![How to Assign Temporary Badge to a User... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/14099788/image-34a479bc.png)
   The Temporary badge is assigned successfully.
   ![How to Assign Temporary Badge to a User... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/14099788/image-4633064d.png)
