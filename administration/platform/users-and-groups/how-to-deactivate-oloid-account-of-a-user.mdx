Steps to Deactivate OloID Account of an User:

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Directory** on the left panel menu.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/8394599/b4cbfd72-e2f9-438d-b14d-d86fe159ae36-8e16c199.png)
   The **Users** page is displayed.
3. Click **Username** to edit.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/8394599/f01600e2-23c6-467f-9108-c4a02d4ed40e-65492525.png)
   The user details page is displayed.
4. On the top-right, go to **Action** > **Deactivate Oloid Account**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/8394599/d1117f64-3eb3-4383-8589-9f20414e89a7-360eda14.png)
   A confirmation pop-up box is displayed.
   ![How to deactivate OloID Account of a... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8394599/image-ca198f9e.png)
5. Select **Block**.
   The user's Oloid account is removed successfully.
   ![How to deactivate OloID Account of a... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8394599/image-aa5f647b.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:** To recover your account you must create Oloid account.
   </Note>

   For more information, see**[How to create OloID account](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8347798-how-to-create-oloid-account)**.
