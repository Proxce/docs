Steps to send invite to download mobile app:

1. Log in to **Oloid Portal.**
2. On the homepage, click **Directory** from the left menu panel.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9716548/3958fc81-6eb8-4ae9-bf4d-1c8314db18f0-1ce19d36.png)
   The **Users** page is displayed.
   ![How to send invite to download mobile... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9716548/image-d18fe4e9.png)
3. Select user(s).
4. Click **Action** > **Send Email** > **Invite to download mobile app.**
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9716548/77be1123-5412-44ee-a140-ec9425d1788d-a840e3fd.png)
   The invite to download mobile app is sent successfully.
