## Introduction

This document provides instructions to remove one or more endpoints from a group in the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Prerequisites

- You must have admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.
- Ensure that the endpoints are already added to the group.

## Steps to Remove Endpoint from Group

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page, click **Groups** on the left panel menu.
   ![How to Remove Endpoint from Group - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8320126/image-56fb7f68.png)
   The **Groups** page is displayed.
3. Click the preferred group.
   ![How to Remove Endpoint from Group - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8320126/image-7e75bf48.png)
   The **Group Details** page is displayed.
   ![How to Remove Endpoint from Group - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8320126/image-1e4f1c34.png)
4. Click **Endpoints** tab and do the following:
   1. Select the endpoint(s) to remove from the group.

      <Note>
        **Note:** If needed, you can select multiple endpoints to delete from group.
      </Note>

   2. Click **Delete** icon.
      ![How to Remove Endpoint from Group - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8320126/image-8e1a4e34.png)
      The **Confirm Deletion** pop-up box is displayed.
   3. Click **Delete**.
      ![How to Remove Endpoint from Group - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/8320126/886c4b5f-4d53-4255-a02b-f13efd635b86-8af9596e.png)
      The selected endpoint(s) is successfully deleted from the Group.
      ![How to Remove Endpoint from Group - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/8320126/image-5eb91fb6.png)
