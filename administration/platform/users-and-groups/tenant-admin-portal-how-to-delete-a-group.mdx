## Introduction

This document provides instructions to delete a group from the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Prerequisite

You must have admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.

## Steps to Delete a Group

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page, click **Groups** on the left menu panel.
   ![How to Delete a Group - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8493877/image-e256ecb6.png)
   The **Groups** page is displayed.
3. Click the preferred group.
   ![How to Delete a Group - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8493877/image-ee902c03.png)
   The Group details page is displayed.
4. Go to **Action** drop-down and select **Delete**.
   ![How to Delete a Group - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8493877/image-96aa0e78.png)
   The confirmation pop-up box is displayed.
5. DO the following:
   1. Enter the **Group Name**.
   2. Enter the **reason** to delete the group.
   3. Click **Delete**.
      ![How to Delete a Group - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8493877/72460b1c-5869-4645-9c5d-8e7c7024075a-14073ba4.png)
      The group is deleted successfully.
      ![How to Delete a Group - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/8493877/image-8e5044ec.png)
