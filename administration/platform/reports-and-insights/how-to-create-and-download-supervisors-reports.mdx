## Introduction

A Supervisors Reports provides supervisors details of all the supervisors of the associated applications, including details regarding connections, assigned applications, assigned connections, and assigned groups.

## Pre-Requisite

You must have a Tenant Admin account.

## Steps to **create & download Supervisors** Reports

1. Log in to the **Oloid Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **Reports > Ad-Hoc** on the left menu panel.
   ![How to create and download Supervisors... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/12807154/image-a5fbe542.png)
   The **Ad-Hoc Reports** page is displayed.
   ![How to create and download Supervisors... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/12807154/image-57aa92a9.png)
3. Use one of the following methods to download the report:
   1. On the top right, click **Create Report** > **Supervisors Reports**.
      ![How to create and download Supervisors... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/12807154/image-ba5c7420.png)
   2. Select Supervisors Report from the left menu panel then click **Create & Download Report.**
      ![How to create and download Supervisors... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/12807154/image-9dd6d206.png)

The **Supervisors Reports** is saved to the local device and listed on the **Ad-Hoc Reports** page under the Supervisors Reports tab.

![How to create and download Supervisors... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/12807154/image-e584aaa0.png)

<Note>
  **Note:**

  - Ensure you have sufficient permissions as a **Tenant Admin** before attempting to create the report.
  - The downloaded file format for reports is XLSX.
</Note>
