**Steps to Deactivate Endpoint:**

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Endpoints** on the left panel menu.
   ![How to deactivate Endpoint in the... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8463355/image-41a3ffe8.png)
   The Endpoints page is displayed.
3. Select **Endpoint Name** to edit.
   ![How to deactivate Endpoint in the... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8463355/image-0ac75c51.png)
   The Endpointdetail page is displayed.
4. Go to **Action** dropdown > select **Deactivate**.
   ![How to deactivate Endpoint in the... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8463355/image-8196aa10.png)
   The **Confirm Deactivation** pop-up box is displayed.
   ![How to deactivate Endpoint in the... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8463355/image-f18a9056.png)
5. Click **Confirm**.
   The endpoint is deactivated and archived.
   ![How to deactivate Endpoint in the... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/8463355/image-01e9117e.png)
