## Introduction

This document provides step-by-step instructions to Deactivate Badge in Oloid User Portal.

## Steps to Deactivate Badge

1. Login to the **Oloid User Portal**.
2. On the homepage, click **Badge**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9880879/ad945229-16c1-4b57-bd25-4f574f614350-42b33288.png)
   The **Badge Credential** page is displayed.
3. Click **Deactivate Badge**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/9880879/9a2ba5de-c630-485b-82e1-b0e894c0362d-742fdf1d.png)
   The confirmation pop-box is displayed.
   ![How to Deactivate Badge in Oloid User... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9880879/image-552fc2bc.png)
4. On the pop-up box, do the following:
   1. Select reason. (Optional)
   2. Click **Deactivate**.
      The badge is deactivated successfully.
      ![How to Deactivate Badge in Oloid User... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9880879/image-d04a4efe.png)
