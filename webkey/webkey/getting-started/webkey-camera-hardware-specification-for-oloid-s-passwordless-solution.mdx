## Introduction

For optimal performance in image capture for Oloid Passwordless Applications , selecting a webcam that meets specific technical criteria is essential. This document outlines the recommended specifications to ensure the webcam delivers clear, accurate, and efficient facial recognition.

---

## Specifications

### Resolution

The webcam resolution plays a key role in capturing high-quality images necessary for Oloid's Passwordless Applications.

- **Minimum resolution**: 720 MP (1280 x 720 pixels) or higher.
- **Recommended resolution**: 1080 MP (1920 x 1080 pixels).

<Note>
  **Note:** 480p cameras will work in certain use cases, please check with your account representative to confirm compatibility.
</Note>

### Frame Rate

Higher frame rates capture more frames per second, providing better detailed facial images.

**Recommended frame rate**: 30 FPS or higher.

### Low-Light Performance

**Details:** Webcam must be capable of capturing usable images in varying lighting conditions, and times of day.

### Lens Type

The choice of lens affects image quality, particularly clarity and focus over different distances.

**Recommended lens type**: fixed focus lens is optimized for standard distances, making it suitable for most typical desktop, laptops, and devices with USB connectivity setups.

## Compression Formats

To reduce storage requirements without losing image quality, appropriate compression formats are essential.

**Recommended formats**: H.264 (HEVC) for better compression with minimal loss of detail.

## Connectivity and Compatibility

## Connectivity

Webcam must support reliable connectivity to the facial recognition system.

- USB 2.0/3.0 compatible
- Plug-and-play functionality, ensuring easy setup without the need for additional drivers or software.

## Compatibility

**Details:** Compatible with major operating systems and video conferencing applications, ensuring smooth integration with Oloid's Passwordless Applications.
