## Introduction

The exitfeature in the **Oloid DeviceLock App** allows admin to safely close the application while in **Admin Mode**. When the app is exited, the admin is automatically logged out and returned to the device home screen.

## Prerequisite

You must have **Tenant Admin access** to the **Oloid Tenant Admin Portal**.

## Steps to Exit the DeviceLock Application

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device.
   ![How to Exit the Oloid DeviceLock App in... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/11484484/image-785b943a.png)
   The welcome screen is displayed.
2. Log in to Admin mode in **DeviceLock** App. For more details refer [How to log in to admin mode in DeviceLock App](https://oloid.help/en/articles/10353022-how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode-in-devicelock-app).

Tap **More option** icon (![How to Exit the Oloid DeviceLock App in... - screenshot 2](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/e4gi378c/1551479200/175cef0b5e13365eb2152438e79a/image.png?expires=1790488800&signature=2da268d2d1467ac2fa7eaa8d3c6aead30467ccf4e09e4184209b7e0cfbee3faf&req=dSUiF815lINfWfMW3nq%2BgSJnnGpeyafhQD60Svchd1KUg7O%2FPF2MCw4Ux%2BAK%0AbsegjnriSPDFGAI9koY53QwdjKo%3D%0A)) on bottom-right side of the screen> Tap **Exit App**.

<Note>
  **Note:** By Tapping **Exit App**, you will be logged out and redirected to the **home** screen of the device.
</Note>

![How to Exit the Oloid DeviceLock App in... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/11484484/image-756c1a22.png)

The Oloid DeviceLock App exited successfully.
