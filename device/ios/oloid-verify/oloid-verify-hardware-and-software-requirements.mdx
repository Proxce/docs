## Hardware requirement for iPad

Supported Models

iPad mini, iPad Pro (11-13 inch) and newer models

Camera

True Depth Camera (Recommended)

Mount and Case for the iPad

Recommended for secure placement and durability

## Software Requirement

The Oloid Verify application requires iOS 15 or later. To ensure compatibility, security, and performance, keep the iPad updated to the most recent supported iOS version.

## Electrical and Power Requirement

- 120V outlet access for charging device.
- PoE adapter for a wired network connection.

## iPad Installation

For iPad installations, the recommended mounting height is 5 feet (approximately 1.5 meters) from the ground. This height is intended to accommodate users of varying heights and provide an optimal authentication experience. For employees who may find the standard mounting height difficult to access, an additional iPad can be installed at a lower height to ensure accessibility and ease of use for all users.

For more information, see Oloid's [iPAD Installation Guide.](https://oloid.help/en/articles/6903976-oloid-connect-verify-ipad-installation)

## Important Considerations

- Ensure there is sufficient lighting around the tablet so that the end user's facial features are clearly visible in the camera. Avoid placing the device in areas with poor lighting, excessive backlighting, or direct sunlight that may affect the quality of the captured image.
- The tablet should be mounted such that the device camera is positioned approximately 5 feet (1.5 meters) above the floor to provide an optimal authentication experience for most users.
- The orientation of the iPad (portrait or landscape) is an important installation consideration, as it directly affects facial authentication performance. The device should be installed in the orientation recommended by Oloid for the specific use case to ensure accurate and consistent user recognition.
