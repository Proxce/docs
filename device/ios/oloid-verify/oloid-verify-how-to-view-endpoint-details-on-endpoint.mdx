## Introduction

The View Endpoint Details feature in the Oloid Verify App allows Tenant Admin and Supervisor to quickly access essential information about the paired endpoint directly from the device.

## Prerequisite

You must pair the Oloid Verify endpoint. To pair, refer **[How to pair an Oloid Verify endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616605-how-to-pair-an-oloid-verify-endpoint).**

## Steps to to view endpoint details

1. Launch the **Oloid Verify** App.
   ![How to View Endpoint Details on Endpoint - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8616247/image-17fd6f50.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more information, refer to [How to Pair an Oloid Verify Endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616605-how-to-pair-an-oloid-verify-endpoint)
   </Note>

   ![How to View Endpoint Details on Endpoint - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8616247/image-a75971c1.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.

Tap **Yes- Continue.**

![How to View Endpoint Details on Endpoint - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8616247/image-04d81a7e.png)

The **Settings** screen along with endpoint details are displayed.

![How to View Endpoint Details on Endpoint - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8616247/image-5742e962.png)

<Note>
  **Note:** Endpoint details can also be viewed in the **Settings tab** after logging in as an Admin in Oloid Verify app. To log in, refer to [How to log in to the admin mode of Oloid Verify App as an Admin or Supervisor](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8671139-how-to-log-in-to-the-admin-mode-of-oloid-verify-app-as-an-admin-or-supervisor).
</Note>
