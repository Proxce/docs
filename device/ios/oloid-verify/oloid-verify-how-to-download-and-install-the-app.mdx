## Introduction

The **Oloid Verify App** allows secure and contactless authentication for workforce management and access control. To begin using the app on your iPad, you must download and install the app from the App Store.

## Prerequisites

##### Before You Begin

- A stable and reliable internet connection is available.
- You have a valid Apple Account (Apple ID).
  - If you do not already have an Apple Account, follow Apple's official guide to create one: [How to Create a New Apple Account (Apple Support)](https://support.apple.com/en-in/108647?device-type=iphone&utm_source=chatgpt.com).
- Verify that your device meets the minimum hardware and software requirements. For more details, refer [Oloid Verify - Hardware and Software Requirements.](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8617034-oloid-verify-hardware-and-software-requirements)

## Steps to download and install the Oloid Verify app

1. Locate the **App Store** on your home screen and tap on it to launch the app.
   ![How to Download and Install the App - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8592228/image-f8f4b869.png)
2. Tap on the **Search** icon located at the bottom right corner of the screen.
   ![How to Download and Install the App - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8592228/image-1a4c8ffd.png)
   The Search bar is displayed.
3. In the search bar at the top of the screen, type **Oloid Verify** and tap on the search result.
   ![How to Download and Install the App - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8592228/image-60783586.png)
4. Select **Oloid Verify** app.

   <Note>
     **Note:** If you are downloading the app for the first time from the App Store, you will see the **Get** button; otherwise, the **download** icon will appear.
   </Note>

   ![How to Download and Install the App - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/8592228/image-f75d92b1.png)
5. Tap the **Get** button located next to the **Oloid Verif**y app icon.
   ![How to Download and Install the App - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/8592228/image-c531c832.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:** If prompted, enter your Apple ID password or use Touch ID/Face ID to confirm the download.
   </Note>

   Once the installation is complete, the **Get** button is changed to **Open** button.
6. Tap on the **Open** button to launch the **Oloid Verify** app.
   ![How to Download and Install the App - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/8592228/image-51dadbee.png)
   The login screen is displayed.
   ![How to Download and Install the App - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/8592228/image-07fadc93.png)
