## Steps to update location

1. Go to **Settings** on **iPad**.
   ![Image](/images/intercom/10288412/6d689ee4-8f12-463f-a5c5-00d3f8bb3223-f3d62ce3.png)
   The Settings menu is displayed.
   ![How to update Location for Oloid Connect - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/10288412/image-723c5786.png)
2. Search **Oloid Connect** ![How to update Location for Oloid Connect - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/10288412/image-6def886f.png) app and open.
   ![How to update Location for Oloid Connect - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/10288412/455c1a4d-8a74-40cb-996f-0bb3b34436a7-3e6288ec.png)
   The **Oloid Connect** app menu is displayed.
3. Tap **Location**.
   ![How to update Location for Oloid Connect - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/10288412/07890f6b-e17d-4cad-bca2-5bf07d85c778-99c10aef.png)
   The **Allow Location Access** menu is displayed.
4. Do one of the following:
   - **Never:** The app cannot use your location, even when you’re using the app.
   - **Ask Next Time Or When I Share**: Every time you open the app, it’ll ask to use your location.
   - **While Using the App:** The app can use your location only when you're using that app.
5. Enable **Precise Location** to allow the app to detect your specific location.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Precise Location option appears when you allow location access.
   </Note>

   The location is updated successfully.
   ![How to update Location for Oloid Connect - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/10288412/da0e487f-99f1-4d2a-8b4b-f28bb5343fa7-a2e3fcb1.png)
