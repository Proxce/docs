On the **Oloid Connect** app, you can share logs with the app developer in two ways:

View and Share App Logs with the Developer
[Upload Logs to the App Developer](#upload-logs-to-the-app-developer)

## Prerequisite

Endpoint configuration for Debug Logs should be enabled in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more information, refer to [How to Enable Debug Logs](https://oloid.help/en/articles/10550185-oloid-connect-how-to-configure-endpoint#h_2b9aa9dc1e:~:text=Enable%20Generate%20Debug%20Logs%20toggle%20%20to%20activate%20log%20generation%20for%20the%20endpoint%20until%20disabled.)

## View and Share Logs with the App Developer

### Steps to View and Share Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect App**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the initial screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616268-how-to-pair-an-oloid-connect-endpoint).
   </Note>

2. Tap the screen four times using a single finger.

   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/13405850/image-00093ad5.png)
   The **OnDevice Sync Report** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Tap **View Logs.**
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/13405850/image-5d38bf12.png)
   The log report is displayed on the screen.
4. On the top right corner of the report page, tap share button to send the log report to the developer.
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/13405850/image-a858baf7.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:** use **AirDrop** to transfer the logs to another iPad or iOS device where the Mail app is configured. Once transferred, you can email the logs from that device.
   </Note>

   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/13405850/image-9be21b54.png)

## Upload Logs to the App Developer

### Steps to Upload Logs

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect App**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the preview screen of the App. For more details, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616268-how-to-pair-an-oloid-connect-endpoint).
   </Note>

   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/13405850/image-00093ad5.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the preview screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes- Continue.**
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/13405850/9a24b866-2972-486d-be7e-a343751fdba2-c6ddcb79.png)
   The **Settings** screen is displayed.
4. Tap **Start** in the **Health Check** section.
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/13405850/ad3e1f5c-df2f-41ec-87de-67ed6d669ad2-dda6a8a1.png)
   The **Please Select Health Type** pop-up box is displayed.
5. Tap **Upload Logs.**
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 8](/images/intercom/13405850/image-ae09a20e.png)
   The logs are successfully uploaded and automatically shared with the app developer.
   ![How to share Logs with App Developer - screenshot 9](/images/intercom/13405850/image-58a4bd08.png)
