## Introduction

This document provides step-by-step instructions to view endpoint details on the Oloid Connect iPhone app.

## Prerequisite

The endpoint must be paired.

For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint on iPhone](https://oloid.help/en/articles/12577230-how-to-pair-an-oloid-connect-endpoint-on-iphone)

## Steps to view Endpoint Details on the Endpoint

1. Launch the **Oloid Connect App**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** Since the endpoint is already paired, the App will launch in the initial screen of the App. For more, refer to [How to pair an Oloid Connect endpoint on iPhone](https://oloid.help/en/articles/12577230-how-to-pair-an-oloid-connect-endpoint-on-iphone)
   </Note>

   ![How to View Endpoint Details on the... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/12585316/image-2089d1c7.png)
2. Double tap with two fingers on the initial screen to launch the Admin Mode.
3. Tap **Yes-Continue.**
   ![How to View Endpoint Details on the... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/12585316/3cc855a2-0c23-4e54-a44d-e1f22f5afbfe-18fed654.png)
   The **Settings** page is displayed.
4. On the Settings page, the endpoint details are displayed.
   ![How to View Endpoint Details on the... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/12585316/58cd31f4-8cf7-44b3-a088-19d177fb89ae-027503e5.png)
