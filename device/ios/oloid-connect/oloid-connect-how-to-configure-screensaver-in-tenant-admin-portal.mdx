## Introduction

This document explains how to configure Screensaver settings for the Oloid Connect application in the Tenant Admin Portal. The configured screensaver is displayed on the application's initial screen.

## Prerequisites

- You must have access to Tenant Admin Portal.
- The **Oloid Connect application** must be created and configured in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more, refer to [How to create and configure Oloid Connect Application in Tenant Admin Portal](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8655943-how-to-create-and-configure-oloid-connect-application-in-tenant-admin-portal)

## Steps to Configure Screensaver

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the **Applications** page, click on the preferred Oloid Connect application.
   ![How to Configure Screensaver in Tenant... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/14493925/9f89c7a2-1c62-4a42-b386-2859701842fc-99aa4506.png)
   The application **details** page is displayed.
   ![How to Configure Screensaver in Tenant... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/14493925/image-ff7a1de6.png)
3. Go to **Theme** tab > Enable **Screensaver** toggle.

   <Note>
     **Note:** When enabled, all screensaver configuration fields become active.
   </Note>

   ![How to Configure Screensaver in Tenant... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/14493925/image-f4199948.png)
4. Under **Select Mode**, select one of the following:
   1. Select **Passive Wake Mode** to show a cover screen while keeping the camera on in the background. The cover screen closes automatically when a face is detected. See Sample screen
   2. Select **Energy Saver Mode** to turn off the camera and show **Resume** button on the screen. Tap on the **Resume** button to turn on the camera again and continue authentication. See Sample screen
5. Under **Trigger Screen-saver after**, set how long the device waits before the selected mode starts. Select the required time in minutes (**Min**) and seconds (**Sec**).
   ![How to Configure Screensaver in Tenant... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/14493925/image-ad685c27.png)
6. Under **Customize Screensaver**, do the following:
   1. Enter **Title**.
   2. Enter **Subtitle** (optional).
   3. Enter **Text Color** using a hex code.
7. Upload images as required:
   1. **Customer Logo** – Click or drag an image to upload.

      <Note>
        **Note:** The **Customer Logo** must meet the required specifications. It should be up to **528 × 144 px**, with a maximum size of **1 MB**, and must be in **SVG or PNG format**.
      </Note>

   2. **Background Image** – Click or drag an image to upload.

      <Note>
        **Note:** The **Background Image** must be up to **1080 × 1920 px** with a maximum size of **2 MB**, and must be in **SVG or PNG format**.
      </Note>

      ![How to Configure Screensaver in Tenant... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/14493925/image-563a06a0.png)

Click **Deploy Changes**.

![How to Configure Screensaver in Tenant... - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/14493925/017c5f23-f45e-495c-968e-1e1bf6402ba2-8dfeb07d.png)

The screensaver is enabled and configured successfully.

![How to Configure Screensaver in Tenant... - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/14493925/image-c395771a.png)
