## Introduction

An Oloid Connect endpoint that is already paired and in use can be deactivated when it is no longer required for active use.

<Note>
  **Note:** Once you pair an endpoint with Oloid Connect application, it gets saved and appears in the Tenant Admin Portal.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- You must have Admin access to the Tenant Admin Portal.
- The Oloid Connect endpoint must be paired and available in the Tenant Admin Portal. For more, refer to[Oloid Connect – How to Pair an Endpoint](https://oloid.help/en/articles/8616268-oloid-connect-how-to-pair-an-endpoint)

## Steps to Deactivate Endpoint

1. Log in to the **Tenant Admin Portal**.
2. On the home page of the portal, click **Endpoints** on the left menu panel.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/16096126/635e3de4-675c-46a8-93f8-37de42aa1b85-013fb478.png)
   The **Endpoints** page is displayed.
3. Select the Oloid Connect Endpoint.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/16096126/image-6a7e595e.png)
   The **Endpoint Details** page is displayed.
   ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/16096126/image-bff9333e.png)
4. Do the following to deactivate the endpoint:
   1. Click **Action**.
   2. From the drop-down menu, click **Deactivate**.
      ![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/16096126/image-ed16089c.png)
      The **Confirm Deactivation** Pop up box is displayed.

Click **Confirm**.

![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/16096126/image-444938fc.png)

The Oloid Connect Endpoint is deactivated successfully.

![How to Deactivate Endpoint in Tenant... - screenshot 6](/images/intercom/16096126/image-3d051ba4.png)
