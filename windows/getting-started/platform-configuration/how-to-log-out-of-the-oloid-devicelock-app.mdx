## Introduction

The **Logout** feature in the **Oloid DeviceLock app** allows administrators and supervisors to securely exit Admin Mode after completing configurations or management tasks. Logging out ensures that unauthorized users cannot access administrative settings on the device.

## Prerequisite

You must have **Admin access** to the **DeviceLock app**.

## Steps to log out of Oloid Devicelock App

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device.
   ![How to Log out of the Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/11501456/image-e0fec7a2.png)
   The welcome screen is displayed.
2. Log in to the **admin mode** in **DeviceLock** App. For more details, refer [How to log in to admin mode in DeviceLock App](https://oloid.help/en/articles/10353022-how-to-log-in-to-admin-mode-in-devicelock-app).
3. Tap **Logout icon** (![How to Log out of the Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/11501456/image-c0fca379.png)) on the top-right side of the screen.

   <Note>
     **Note:** You can exit **Admin Mode** from any screen, as the top header with the Logout icon remains fixed and always accessible.
   </Note>

   ![How to Log out of the Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/11501456/image-4f33fa3f.png)
   The **Confirm Logout** pop-up box is displayed.

Tap **Logout**.

![How to Log out of the Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/11501456/image-de91812b.png)

You will be redirected to the **Welcome screen**.

![How to Log out of the Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/11501456/image-e8ed5001.png)

You have successfully logged out from the DeviceLock application.
