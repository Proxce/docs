## Introduction

The **Login with Badge and PIN** feature in the **Oloid DeviceLock App** allows users to securely access their **Workforce/HRMS platform** through a two-step authentication process. Users first tap their **Badge** on the device to verify their identity, followed by entering their **PIN** to complete the login.

## Prerequisites

- Ensure that the Android device is connected to an external card reader.
- You must have a valid Okta account.
- You must have your assigned Badge ID.
- You must have your assigned PIN.

## Steps to Log in with Badge and Pin

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.

   <Note>
     **Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.
   </Note>

   The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with Badge**.
   ![How to Log in to Workforce/HRMS... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/8403260/image-71bc9fc8.png)
   The **Scan Badge at the Reader** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Tap the Badge on the Badge Reader to scan it.
   ![How to Log in to Workforce/HRMS... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/8403260/image-d7127499.png)
   After successful Badge authentication, the **Enter PIN** pop-up box is displayed.

Enter **Pin**.

![How to Log in to Workforce/HRMS... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/8403260/image-2cd06c73.png)

After successful PIN authentication, you are signed in to the Okta system.
