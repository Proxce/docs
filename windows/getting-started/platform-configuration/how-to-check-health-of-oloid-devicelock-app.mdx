**Steps to check health:**

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device.
   ![How to check health of Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/11508048/image-e0fec7a2.png)
2. Do any one of the following:

Select **More Option** icon (![How to check health of Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 2](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/e4gi378c/1551479200/175cef0b5e13365eb2152438e79a/image.png?expires=1790488800&signature=2da268d2d1467ac2fa7eaa8d3c6aead30467ccf4e09e4184209b7e0cfbee3faf&req=dSUiF815lINfWfMW3nq%2BgSJnnGpeyafhQD60Svchd1KUg7O%2FPF2MCw4Ux%2BAK%0AbsegjnriSPDFGAI9koY53QwdjKo%3D%0A)) on bottom-right side of the screen.
      OR
   2. Double tap on the screen.
      ![How to check health of Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/11508048/image-78274819.png)
      The Enter Admin Mode pop-up box is displayed.
3. Select **Continue**.
   ![How to check health of Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/11508048/image-09c4e563.png)
4. Select **Start** to initiate the **health check**.
   ![How to check health of Oloid DeviceLock... - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/11508048/image-1a0d73f1.png)
   Logs are successfully shared with Firebase.
