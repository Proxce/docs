**Steps to share logs:**

1. Launch **Oloid DeviceLock App** on your device.
   ![How to share logs in DeviceLock App - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/11507892/image-e0fec7a2.png)
2. Do any one of the following:
   1. Select **More Option** icon (![How to share logs in DeviceLock App - screenshot 2](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/e4gi378c/1551479200/175cef0b5e13365eb2152438e79a/image.png?expires=1790488800&signature=2da268d2d1467ac2fa7eaa8d3c6aead30467ccf4e09e4184209b7e0cfbee3faf&req=dSUiF815lINfWfMW3nq%2BgSJnnGpeyafhQD60Svchd1KUg7O%2FPF2MCw4Ux%2BAK%0AbsegjnriSPDFGAI9koY53QwdjKo%3D%0A)) on bottom-right side of the screen.
      OR
   2. Double tap on the screen.
      ![How to share logs in DeviceLock App - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/11507892/image-40dde445.png)
      The **Enter Admin Mode** pop-up box is displayed.
3. Select **Continue**.
   ![How to share logs in DeviceLock App - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/11507892/image-f7858249.png)
4. Select **Send**.
   ![How to share logs in DeviceLock App - screenshot 5](/images/intercom/11507892/image-9b48efe8.png)
5. Enter the recipient email address > click **Send** icon (![How to share logs in DeviceLock App - screenshot 6](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/e4gi378c/1555045181/318f36bfcea277a306612290d3f7/image.png?expires=1790488800&signature=ecdbfe85a5ddbbd12d5c0228d64937e851ca250f836111b0031fd4f8e7558032&req=dSUiE8l6mIBXWPMW3nq%2BgUDf3071Sw7wN7Car5gECFT2tIV5gBK6STykUXxl%0A14eHUoJOyZfbraanQdeE7Ke8xbk%3D%0A)) on the top-right side of the screen.
   ![How to share logs in DeviceLock App - screenshot 7](/images/intercom/11507892/image-96d13495.png)
   The DeviceLock logs file is successfully sent to the recipient's email address.
