## Introduction

The **Login with QR Code** feature in the **Oloid DeviceLock App** allows users to securely access their **Workforce/HRMS platform** by scanning a QR code linked to their **credentials**. This method provides a quick and secure way to authenticate without entering a password.

## Prerequisites

- You must have a valid Okta account.
- A valid QR code must be assigned to you.

## Steps to Log in with QR Code

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.

   <Note>
     **Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.
   </Note>

   The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with QR**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR Code and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears.
   </Note>

   ![How to Log into Workforce/HRMS... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/10026016/image-b92acf19.png)
   The camera turns on.
3. Show your QR Code in front of the camera to capture the QR code.
   ![How to Log into Workforce/HRMS... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/10026016/image-2f8ca69b.png)
   After successful authentication, you are signed in to the Okta system.
   ![How to Log into Workforce/HRMS... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/10026016/f6f6a9fb-c2f1-46aa-a077-e0baf7dbef39-3ecf8d92.png)
