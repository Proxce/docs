## Introduction

The **Oloid DeviceLock app** enables users to securely unlock their Android devices using **NFC Tag credential**. After successful authentication, the device unlocks and redirects to the device **home screen**.

## Prerequisites

- Android device must be connected to an external NFC reader/card reader.

  <Note>
    **Note:** If the Android device has a **built-in NFC reader**, you can scan the tag directly on the device.
  </Note>

- You must have your assigned NFC ID.

## Steps to Unlock the Device

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.

   <Note>
     **Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.
   </Note>

   The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with NFC Tag**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** You will only see the authentication methods (Face, PIN, QR Code, Password and NFC) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears.
   </Note>

   ![How to Unlock the Device Screen Using... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/12779720/image-944029c7.png)
   **Tap NFC Tag at the device** pageis displayed.

Scan the NFC Tag.

![How to Unlock the Device Screen Using... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/12779720/image-f80611ef.png)

After successful authentication, the device unlocks and displays the **home screen**.
