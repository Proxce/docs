## Introduction

The **Oloid DeviceLock app** enables users to securely unlock their Android devices using **Badge credential**. After successful authentication, the device unlocks and redirects to the device **home screen**.

## Prerequisites

- Ensure that the Android device is connected to an external card reader.
- You must have your assigned Badge ID.

## Steps to Unlock the Device

1. Press the **Power** button or tap the screen on your device.

   <Note>
     **Note:** The Device Lock app is displayed automatically, as it always runs in Kiosk Mode.
   </Note>

   The Welcome screen is displayed.
2. Tap **Login with Badge**.

   <Note>
     **Note:** You will only see the authentication methods (Face, Badge, PIN, QR, Password and NFC/ID) enabled for you. Button names may vary based on configuration. Contact [Support](mailto:Support@oloid.ai) if no authentication method appears.
   </Note>

   ![How to Unlock the Device Screen using... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/12779676/image-71bc9fc8.png)
   The **Scan Badge at the Reader** pop-up box is displayed.

Tap the Badge on the Badge Reader to scan it.

![How to Unlock the Device Screen using... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/12779676/image-d7127499.png)

After successful Badge authentication, the device unlocks and displays the **home screen**.
