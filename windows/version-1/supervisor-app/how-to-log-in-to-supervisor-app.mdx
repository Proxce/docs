## Steps to log in to Supervisor App

1. Double-click on the **Supervisor App** to start.
   ![How to log in to Supervisor App - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9037188/image-a8da1205.png)
2. Enter your login credentials in the appropriate fields.
   ![How to log in to Supervisor App - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9037188/image-be702523.png)

   <Note>
     **Note:** If this is your first login after installing the application, please enter your Tenant Name and pair the endpoint.
   </Note>

   Refer [How to pair endpoint using Pair as Supervisor in Supervisor App](https://oloid.help/en/articles/9059601-how-to-pair-endpoint-using-pair-as-supervisor-in-supervisor-app)
3. Click **Login**.
   The Admin Home screen is displayed.
   ![How to log in to Supervisor App - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9037188/image-ff61a91c.png)
