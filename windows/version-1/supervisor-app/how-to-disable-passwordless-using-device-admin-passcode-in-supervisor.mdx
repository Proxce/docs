**Pre-requisite**

The Supervisor app is already paired. Contact your Admin for the passcode.

For details, refer[How to create device-admin passcode for Supervisor App](https://oloid.help/en/articles/9244629-how-to-create-device-admin-passcode-for-supervisor-app).

To disable passwordless:

1. Double-click on **Supervisor** app.
   The app opens.
2. Do the following:
   1. Click **More Options** > **Disable Passwordless**.
      ![How to disable Passwordless using... - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/9153890/image-5118129e.png)
      The **Disable Passwordless** screen is displayed.
   2. Enter the **Device-Admin Passcode**.

      <Note>
        **Note:** The Device-Admin Passcode is created in the Oloid Portal.
      </Note>

      ![How to disable Passwordless using... - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/9153890/image-abcf6c59.png)
   3. Click **Disable Passwordless**.
      ![How to disable Passwordless using... - screenshot 3](/images/intercom/9153890/image-aa0f66db.png)
      The Passwordless is disabled successfully.
      ![How to disable Passwordless using... - screenshot 4](/images/intercom/9153890/image-321dcbec.png)
      The Supervisor app is no longer available on the Windows lock screen.
