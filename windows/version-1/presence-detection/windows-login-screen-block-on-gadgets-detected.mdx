## Screen block on Gadgets detected

Your windows system supports the screen block on "**Gadget Detected**" functionality with Oloid's Presence detection feature.

The Windows system will automatically get blocked if the camera detects any other device in use other than your work system. The screen displays a screen block message.

<Note>
  **Note:** All screen interactions are disabled, except for the buttons shown on the blocked screen.
</Note>

![Screen block on Gadgets detected - screenshot 1](/images/intercom/10021367/Gadget-detection-224eaa20.gif)

## Requirement for Presence Detection Feature

1. Your Windows system must be registered for Presence Detection.
2. The "Gadget Detected" option must be enabled by your admin at the endpoint level.

## Steps to Take if Your Screen is Blocked

![Screen block on Gadgets detected - screenshot 2](/images/intercom/10021367/image-0911c7fa.png)

1. **Click "Get Help" to contact your Support team and report screen block.**
   - The Support Team will notify your supervisor of the event.
   - The supervisor will review and unblock your screen.
2. **Click "Unblock Now" after Supervisor confirms of screen unblock.**
   - Your screen will be unblocked, and you can resume work.
3. **If the screen remains blocked and the "Unblock Failed" popup appears:**
   - Contact your supervisor to reach out to Oloid Support.

<Note>
  **Note:** Without Supervisor approval, your screen will automatically be unblocked after the specified time duration if auto unblock is enabled for that endpoint.
</Note>

For more information on auto unblock, see [How the Self-Unblock Feature Works in Windows Login Presence Detection](https://oloid.help/en/articles/10319146-how-the-self-unblock-feature-works-in-windows-login-presence-detection)
